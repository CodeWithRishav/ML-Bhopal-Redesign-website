# AI Day Bhopal 2023: A Resounding Success

AI Day Bhopal 2023 marked a historic milestone for the ML community in Central India. On April 15, 2023, over 300 machine learning enthusiasts, researchers, students, and industry professionals gathered in Bhopal for what would become the foundation of a thriving annual tradition.

## A Day of Learning and Inspiration

The event featured an packed agenda that balanced deep technical content with accessible introductions for newcomers. From morning keynotes that set the stage with a vision for AI's future, to afternoon hands-on workshops where attendees built real models, AI Day 2023 demonstrated that Bhopal had the talent and enthusiasm to become a major AI hub in Central India.

Speakers from across the industry shared their journeys — from students who had landed AI roles at top companies, to researchers pushing the boundaries of what's possible with machine learning. The energy in the room was electric, with every session followed by lively Q&A discussions that stretched into the breaks.

## Workshops That Made Learning Real

One of the highlights was the hands-on TensorFlow workshop, where participants built image classification models from scratch. Many attendees shared that it was their first time training a neural network, and the guided environment made it approachable and fun. The workshop format — with mentors circulating to help debug code in real time — became a template we've replicated at every major event since.

## A Community is Born

Perhaps the most significant outcome of AI Day 2023 wasn't any single talk or workshop, but the connections forged between attendees. Teams that met at AI Day went on to collaborate on projects, enter hackathons together, and some even founded startups. The Commudle community group that launched alongside AI Day 2023 quickly grew to hundreds of members, seeding what is now a 3,500+ strong ML Bhopal community.

## Looking Ahead

The success of AI Day 2023 gave us the confidence to dream bigger. It proved that Bhopal's technical community was ready for world-class AI events, and inspired us to plan even more ambitious editions. AI Day has since grown to include 500+ attendees, Google Developer Experts, dedicated AI-Thon competitions, and partnerships with leading colleges across Central India. The journey that began in April 2023 continues to accelerate.
